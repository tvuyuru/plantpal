Welcome to PlantPal. It is a simple place to keep every houseplant you own, with its type, its last watering date and how often it needs water.

When a plant looks unwell, you will be able to describe the symptoms, upload a photo and get a likely cause with next steps, such as overwatering, underwatering, a soil problem or too little light.

This is an early version of the site, built as a class project and still growing.
